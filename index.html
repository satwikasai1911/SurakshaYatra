About the data and method

Maps and places. Map tiles © Esri and contributors. Place search and nearby places © OpenStreetMap contributors. Routes come from the public OSRM demo server, which is meant for light use.

Accident data. The page uses a CSV of real records when one is loaded. Without one it shows clearly labelled generated samples. Starting points for real data: state-wise top ten black spots (data.gov.in) and the MoRTH report "Road Accidents in India".

Risk score. Records within 2.5 km of a route are counted with weights (minor 1, grievous 2, fatal 3), divided by route length and mapped to 0–100. It compares routes against each other. It is indicative and does not guarantee safety.

Time. Estimated average driving time from the routing service. It is not live traffic.
